Know before converting

JPG cannot preserve transparency. Transparent pixels use your chosen background. Browser conversion exports the first frame of animated images.

Choosing JPG, PNG, or WebP

Format guide

  • JPG: photographs and broad compatibility; no transparency.
  • PNG: graphics, screenshots, and transparent backgrounds.
  • WebP: smaller web delivery with transparency in modern browsers.

Before you convert

Conversion creates a new file and leaves the original unchanged. Transparent areas use the selected background when converting to JPG. Animated input becomes one still frame.

Confirm colours, transparency, and sharpness before replacing an original or sending a file to print.
