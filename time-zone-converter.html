About time zone conversion

This tool uses your browser's built-in time zone database, which follows the same official IANA time zone rules used by phones and computers — including daylight saving time transitions, so a converted time will automatically reflect whether each region observes DST on that particular date.

Frequently asked questions

Does this account for daylight saving time?
Yes — because the conversion uses the standard IANA time zone database, it automatically applies the correct daylight saving offset (or lack of it) for the specific date you enter, not just a fixed hour difference.

Can I compare more than two time zones at once?
Yes, add as many target time zones as you need from the dropdown — each one gets its own row showing the converted date and time, which is useful for scheduling a call across several countries at once.

Why does the date sometimes shift to the next or previous day?
Time zones can be many hours apart, so converting a late evening time in one zone can land on the following morning somewhere else — and vice versa. Each result row shows the actual converted date alongside the time to make this clear.
