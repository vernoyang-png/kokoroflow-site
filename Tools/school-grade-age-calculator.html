How it works

  1. Enter the birthdate. The tool calculates the child's age as of your chosen cutoff date.
  2. Set the age cutoff. This is the date by which a child must reach a certain age to start that grade — it varies by state and country, so check your district's actual policy if you're not sure.
  3. Read the estimate. The result uses the common US age-to-grade mapping (age 5 by the cutoff → Kindergarten, and so on). Actual placement can differ due to redshirting, school policy, or academic assessment.

This is a general estimate based on typical US age cutoffs, not an official placement. Individual schools and districts make their own enrollment decisions — confirm with your child's school for anything that matters, like enrollment deadlines.
