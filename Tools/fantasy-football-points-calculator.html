How it works

  1. Pick a scoring preset. Standard, Half-PPR, or Full PPR sets the points-per-reception value — the most common thing that varies between leagues.
  2. Enter the stat line. Fill in whichever categories apply; anything left at 0 just won't contribute.
  3. Adjust point values if your league is custom. Every "Pts each" field is editable, so you can match an unusual scoring system exactly.

All calculations happen in your browser — no stats are uploaded or logged anywhere.
