How it works

  1. Paste any text. It can be messy — the extractor scans the whole thing and picks out anything shaped like an email address.
  2. Choose your options. Remove duplicates, normalize to lowercase, and sort, then pick the output format that fits where you're pasting it.
  3. Copy or download. Use the copy button, or save the list as a plain text or CSV file.

Everything runs in your browser — the text you paste is never uploaded or stored. Only use extracted addresses in ways that respect privacy and anti-spam laws, such as CAN-SPAM in the US: a list of addresses is not the same as permission to email them.
