How it works

  1. Choose the language. Picking the right language noticeably improves accuracy — OCR models are trained per-language.
  2. Upload an image. Clear, well-lit, reasonably high-resolution images work best. Very small or blurry text is harder to read accurately.
  3. Extract, then copy. The text appears in the box above once processing finishes — review it, since OCR isn't perfect, especially on handwriting or unusual fonts.

Your image is processed entirely in your browser using an open-source OCR engine (Tesseract.js) — it is never uploaded to KokoroFlow or any other server. The first extraction on a given language may take a little longer while the recognition model downloads.
