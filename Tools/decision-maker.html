How it works

  1. List your options. One per line — two or more, no limit on length or count.
  2. Spin the wheel. Each option becomes a slice; the wheel spins and the pointer lands on your answer.
  3. Share it if you like. The copy-link button encodes your current list into the URL — anyone who opens it sees the same wheel and can spin it themselves.

Your options stay in this page — nothing is uploaded or logged. The share link just encodes your list into the URL itself, so closing the tab leaves no record anywhere.
