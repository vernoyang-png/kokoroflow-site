Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when you use KokoroFlow.

Calculator information

Most calculator inputs are processed directly in your browser. KokoroFlow does not require an account and does not intentionally store the financial, health, travel, or work figures you enter into these calculators.

Server and usage data

Our hosting provider and security services may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested page, referring page, timestamps, and error or security logs. This information may be used to operate, secure, and improve the website.

Cookies, advertising, and Google services

KokoroFlow may use Google AdSense and related Google advertising services after approval. Google and other third-party vendors may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons, IP addresses, and similar identifiers to serve, measure, personalize, or limit advertising.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can manage advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings and learn about industry opt-out choices at AboutAds.

Consent requirements

Where required by law, visitors may be shown a consent message before personalized advertising or certain storage technologies are used. Advertising choices may depend on your location and selections.

External services and links

Some tools may request public reference data, such as exchange rates, from third-party services. External websites have their own privacy policies, and KokoroFlow is not responsible for their practices.

Children’s privacy

KokoroFlow is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website or applicable requirements change. Questions can be sent to hello@kokoroflow.net.
