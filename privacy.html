Privacy policy

Your privacy at KokoroFlow

This policy explains what information may be processed when you use kokoroflow.net.

Last updated: 6 August 2026

1. Information you provide

If you email us, we receive the information you include, such as your email address, name, and message. Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information.

2. Tool inputs and selected files

Most KokoroFlow calculators run directly in your browser. We do not intentionally collect the amounts, text, passwords, or other values you enter into these tools. Image and PDF tools marked “No upload” process selected files locally in your browser; those files are not sent to or stored by KokoroFlow.

The PDF processing library is hosted on KokoroFlow and runs in your browser. See our Open-Source Licenses. A normal request for the page and its files is still visible to the website hosting provider, but the contents of the PDF or image you select are not included in that request.

3. Technical and usage data

Our hosting provider may process standard server information such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested page, referring page, and the date and time of a request. This information may be used for security, reliability, troubleshooting, and aggregate traffic measurement.

4. Cookies and consent

KokoroFlow may use essential storage needed for site operation. Before non-essential advertising or analytics storage is used where consent is legally required, visitors will be offered the applicable choices. For visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, any Google advertising setup must use a Google-certified consent management platform where required.

5. Advertising and third-party services

KokoroFlow may use Google AdSense to fund free access to the site. Ads may not appear on every page or for every visitor. When advertising is active, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, browser and device information, advertising identifiers, cookies or similar storage, consent choices, and interactions with ads, subject to the available consent settings and their own policies. Selected image and PDF file contents are not provided to advertisers by these tools.

We do not place ads inside file-selection controls, beside download buttons in a way intended to cause accidental clicks, or present ads as tool results. Links to other websites are governed by the privacy policies of those websites.

6. Affiliate links

Some pages may contain clearly identified affiliate links. Clicking such a link may allow the merchant or affiliate network to record a referral. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

7. Data retention and security

We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records, or meet legal obligations. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to reduce unnecessary collection and protect the information we handle.

8. Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection, or restriction of personal data. To make a privacy enquiry, email hello@kokoroflow.net. We may need to verify the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when services or legal requirements change. The updated date will appear at the top of this page.
