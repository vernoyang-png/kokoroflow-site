What problem does this trip budget calculator solve?

It adds up transportation, lodging, food, activities, and other costs across a group of travelers and a trip length, then applies a safety buffer, so you have one practical total to plan around before booking rather than tracking every cost separately.

How to build a trip budget

  1. Enter the number of travelers and trip length in days.
  2. Enter your total transportation cost.
  3. Enter lodging cost per night, and food and activity costs per person per day.
  4. Add any other expected costs, such as insurance or transit passes.
  5. Set a safety buffer percentage and calculate your total.

Privacy, inputs, and limits

  • Upload: No files or booking details are uploaded; the calculation runs in your browser.
  • Input: Traveler count, trip days, and per-category cost estimates.
  • Output: A planning total, not a live quote.
  • Actual prices vary by destination, season, and booking timing.
  • Confirm real transportation, lodging, and activity prices before you book.

Example

Two travelers on a 7-day trip with $900 in transportation, $180-per-night lodging, $65 per person per day for food, $40 per person per day for activities, $200 in other costs, and a 10% safety buffer comes to roughly $4,015 total — about $2,008 per traveler and $574 per day. Raising the buffer to 15-20% is a reasonable adjustment for destinations with less predictable pricing or a first-time visit.

Frequently asked questions

What should go under "other costs"?

Airport transfers, parking, resort fees, tips, local transit passes, travel insurance, mobile data, and currency exchange fees are commonly missed costs that fit well under this category.

How big should my safety buffer be?

10% is a reasonable starting point for a well-planned trip with mostly booked costs. For destinations with variable pricing, last-minute bookings, or a first-time visit, 15-20% gives more breathing room.

Does this include currency conversion?

No. Enter costs in a single currency you're comfortable planning in, and use a separate currency converter if you need to translate local prices first.

Can I use this for a solo trip?

Yes. Set travelers to 1 and the calculator will still split transportation and other shared costs the same way, while food and activity costs scale per person as usual.
