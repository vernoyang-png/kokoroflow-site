Reduce JPG, PNG, or WebP file size without uploading your images. Choose quality and maximum dimensions, then download the optimized copy.

No uploadNo sign-upNo watermark

Privacy

Processing happens in your browser. Your images are not sent to KokoroFlow or stored on a server.

Limits

  • Very large images may exceed your device memory.
  • PNG ignores the quality slider; choose WebP or JPG for stronger compression.
  • Animated images are exported as a single frame.

How to compress an image responsibly

Choose the right settings

  1. Add JPG, PNG, or WebP images.
  2. Start at 80% quality and a 2,400-pixel maximum for web use.
  3. Choose WebP for smaller web images, JPG for broad compatibility, or PNG when transparency matters.
  4. Inspect the result before deleting your original.

What changes

Compression may reduce pixel dimensions and re-encode the visible image. It does not improve a blurry source, and strong compression can create blocks or soft edges. PNG ignores the quality control because it is lossless.

Files remain in your browser, but very large batches can exceed mobile memory.
