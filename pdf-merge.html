    What problem does PDF Merge solve?

    It combines several PDF documents into one file in the order you choose. This is useful for joining scanned pages, reports, receipts, application documents, or chapters without uploading them to a remote service.

    How to merge PDFs

    1. Choose two or more PDF files.
    2. Use the controls to place them in the required order.
    3. Enter a clear output filename.
    4. Select Merge PDFs.
    5. Download and verify the complete document.

    Privacy, formats, and limits

    • Upload: PDFs are processed locally in your browser.
    • Input/output: PDF only.
    • Size: No server limit; total pages, embedded images, and available device memory determine practical capacity.
    • Password-protected or damaged PDFs may fail.
    • Digital signatures, forms, attachments, links, or other interactive features may not survive unchanged.

    Example

    Combine cover-letter.pdf, resume.pdf, and portfolio.pdf in that order to create one application PDF. Open the result and check every page before sending it.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. Processing is designed to happen on your device. Closing or refreshing the page clears the current selection.

    Can it unlock a protected PDF?

    No. The tool does not remove passwords or access restrictions.

    Will a digital signature remain valid?

    Creating a new combined PDF can invalidate signatures. Keep the originals and verify signed or regulated documents separately.

    What's the fastest way to turn paper documents into PDFs before merging?

    For a few pages, a phone scanning app is enough. If you digitize receipts, contracts or reports every week, a sheet-fed document scanner with an automatic document feeder can scan a whole stack straight to PDF, which you can then combine here in the order you need.
