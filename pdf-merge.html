PDF Merge

Combine several PDFs into one document. Arrange the files in the order you want before merging.

No uploadNo sign-upLocal processing

    Privacy

    PDF content stays in your browser. KokoroFlow does not receive or store your documents.

    Limits

    Password-protected or damaged PDFs may not open. Large files can exceed mobile device memory. Interactive forms, signatures, attachments, and special PDF features may not survive merging.

    The open-source PDF processing code is hosted by KokoroFlow and runs locally in your browser. See open-source licenses.

    Before merging important PDFs

    Recommended workflow

    1. Add PDFs in reading order.
    2. Use the arrows to rearrange them.
    3. Save the new document under a different name.
    4. Verify pages, forms, links, signatures, and attachments.

    Limits and rights

    This tool copies pages into a new PDF. It does not unlock protected files. Signatures may no longer validate, and interactive elements may not survive.

    Only process documents you own or are authorised to use. Keep originals when contracts, records, or compliance evidence matter.
