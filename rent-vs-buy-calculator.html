This model compares net cost after your chosen number of years, treating your down payment and closing costs as money that could otherwise have been invested (opportunity cost), and netting out estimated home equity and selling costs if you buy. It's a planning estimate, not a guarantee — actual rates, appreciation, and costs vary by market and year.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include the opportunity cost of my down payment?

Yes. It applies the return you enter to the money that would otherwise go into the down payment and closing costs.

What costs of owning are included?

Mortgage payments, property tax, maintenance and insurance, buying closing costs, and selling costs when you leave.

Is my data stored?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.
