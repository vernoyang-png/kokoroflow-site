PDF Split & Reorder

Extract selected pages or build a new page order. Use ranges like 1-3, 7, 5.

No uploadExtract pagesCustom order
Use a hyphen for a range and commas between selections. Repeating a page duplicates it.

How it works

For a 10-page PDF, enter 1-4 to split out the first four pages, 10, 1 to reverse those two pages, or 1-10 to keep all pages.

Privacy and limits

The file is processed locally and not uploaded. Large documents may exceed mobile memory. Links, forms, signatures, attachments, and advanced features may not be preserved.

The open-source PDF processing code is hosted by KokoroFlow and runs locally in your browser. See open-source licenses.

What problem does PDF Split & Reorder solve?

It creates a new PDF containing only the pages you need, in the order you specify. You can extract a range, remove unwanted pages, rearrange pages, or duplicate a page.

How to split or reorder a PDF

  1. Choose one PDF document.
  2. Review the detected page count.
  3. Enter pages or ranges, such as 1-3, 7, 5.
  4. Create the new PDF.
  5. Download it and verify page order and content.

Privacy, formats, and limits

  • Upload: The PDF stays in your browser.
  • Input/output: PDF only.
  • Size: No fixed upload limit; large scanned documents may exceed mobile memory.
  • Password-protected and damaged PDFs may not open.
  • Forms, signatures, attachments, bookmarks, and links may change in the new file.

Example

Entering 1-3, 8, 5 creates a five-page PDF containing original pages 1, 2, 3, 8, and 5 in that exact order.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove one page?

Create a page list that excludes it. For a 10-page PDF, use 1-4, 6-10 to omit page 5.

Can I duplicate a page?

Yes. Repeat its number, such as 1, 2, 2, 3.

Does this change the original?

No. It creates a separate PDF. Keep the original until you have verified the new document.
