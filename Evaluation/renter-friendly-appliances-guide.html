Renter-Friendly Appliance Guide

Most appliance advice assumes you own the place. This guide is built around the opposite constraint: nothing drilled, nothing permanently plumbed or vented, everything you can take with you when you move. Three categories, each with a full ranked breakdown.

"No install" still usually means a faucet adapter, a water tank you refill by hand, or a hose near a window — not literally zero setup. Always check your lease for restrictions on portable appliances before buying.
