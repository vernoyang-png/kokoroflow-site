Best Portable Air Conditioners (No Window Kit Needed)

Every unit here cools a room without a permanent window installation — most still use a hose vented near a cracked window, but nothing is drilled, mounted, or left behind when you move.

"No window installation" means no permanently mounted unit — most portable ACs still vent a hose through a window using an included adjustable panel kit, which you remove when you're done. A true zero-venting option is included below for units where even that isn't possible.

08 · Ventless

Evaporative Cooler (No-Hose Type, e.g. ZFYXGS-style)

True zero-venting option

Not a true compressor-based AC — these evaporative coolers use a water tank and cooling pad instead of a compressor, so there's no hose or window kit at all. They work best in dry climates and cool less aggressively than a real AC; treat this as a fan-plus-humidity upgrade, not a BTU-for-BTU replacement.

No compressor, water tank + cooling pad, no venting required, budget-tier pricing.

Sources and limitations

Sources checked September 13, 2026. BTU ratings, EER figures, and exact pricing change often — confirm the current listing before buying.
