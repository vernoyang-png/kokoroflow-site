Apple MacBook Air 13-inch M5 (2026)

Best overall for general coursework

Best for majors

Literature, Languages, Education, Business, Communications, Social Sciences, and general coursework

Why we picked it

A compact everyday choice for papers, research, presentations, and online classes when your course software supports macOS.

Recommended configuration

M5 / 16GB unified memory / 512GB SSD. Consider 24GB for heavier multitasking.

Key specs

Apple M5; 13.6-inch 2560 × 1664 Liquid Retina display; 1.23kg; two Thunderbolt 4 ports, MagSafe 3, headphone jack.

Watch out for

Confirm macOS support for required applications. Memory and internal storage cannot be upgraded after purchase; adapters may be needed for USB-A or HDMI.

ASUS Zenbook A14 UX3407

Lightweight Windows choice

Best for majors

Business, Finance, Marketing, Education, Languages, and Social Sciences

Why we picked it

Its portable design and OLED display make it a useful fit for lecture notes, reading, spreadsheets, and presentations.

Recommended configuration

Snapdragon X Plus / 16GB RAM / 512GB SSD as a target configuration; verify the full UX3407 suffix.

Key specs

Snapdragon processor family; integrated Adreno graphics; 14-inch OLED options; USB4 Type-C, USB-A, HDMI and audio. Processor, panel, memory and weight vary by SKU.

Watch out for

Lenovo LOQ 15

Dedicated graphics for engineering and 3D

Best for majors

Mechanical Engineering, Architecture, Civil Engineering, CAD, 3D, Game Development, and STEM

Why we picked it

Choose a dedicated NVIDIA GPU configuration when coursework includes 3D modeling, rendering, or game development.

Recommended configuration

Target an RTX 5050 configuration with 16GB RAM and 1TB SSD; consider 32GB for larger CAD projects. Confirm the CPU and exact model.

Key specs

15-inch-class Windows gaming laptop family; CPU, GPU, display, ports and weight vary by generation. The manufacturer reference is an example LOQ 15IRX10 family, not proof of the Amazon SKU.

Apple MacBook Pro 14-inch M5

Creative work and development

Best for majors

Graphic Design, Photography, Film & Video, Music Production, Computer Science, and Data Science

Why we picked it

A high-quality display and broad media capabilities suit creative coursework and development in a macOS-compatible workflow.

Recommended configuration

M5 / 16GB unified memory / 512GB SSD minimum; consider 24GB or 32GB and 1TB for media projects or larger datasets.
