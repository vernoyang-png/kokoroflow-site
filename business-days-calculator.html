What counts?

By default, Monday through Friday are business days. Saturday and Sunday are excluded. Holiday schedules vary by country, region, employer, and industry, so this tool only excludes holidays you enter.

Limits

Results are planning estimates, not legal deadline advice. Confirm court, banking, government, shipping, and contract deadlines with the responsible organization.

How business-day counting varies

Choose the counting rule

Some schedules include both dates; others start on the next business day. Select the required method and add known holidays in YYYY-MM-DD format. The tool otherwise excludes only Saturday and Sunday.

The add-days feature moves forward or backward while skipping excluded dates.

Important deadline limits

Courts, banks, carriers, employers, markets, and agencies may define business days differently. Local holidays, closures, time zones, filing cut-offs, and extension rules can change the answer.

Use this for planning and confirm legal, tax, contract, payment, filing, and delivery deadlines with the responsible organisation.
