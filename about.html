About KokoroFlow

Useful calculations, explained clearly.

KokoroFlow creates free, straightforward tools for common decisions involving money, work, health, travel, and daily life.

Why we built KokoroFlow

Everyday calculations should not require an account, a complicated spreadsheet, or a sales call. Our goal is to make practical estimates easy to understand on both desktop and mobile devices.

How our tools are created

Each calculator states its assumptions, shows the important result details, and identifies major limitations. When a calculation depends on public rules or official rates, we link to relevant sources and review time-sensitive inputs.

Our editorial principles

  • Write for people first, not just search engines.
  • Keep advertising visually separate from calculator controls.
  • Do not present estimates as guaranteed outcomes.
  • Correct material errors when they are identified.
  • Respect visitors’ privacy and minimize unnecessary data collection.

Who the tools are for

KokoroFlow is designed primarily for adults in the United States who want a fast planning estimate before making a decision or consulting a qualified professional.

Important limitations

Our financial and health calculators are educational tools. They do not replace personalized tax, legal, investment, lending, nutrition, or medical advice.
